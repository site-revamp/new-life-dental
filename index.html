Welcome to your dental home

A welcoming space.
A personal conversation.

Visiting the dentist can bring questions and uncertainty. Newlife's approach centres on patient education and a caring environment for individuals and families.

One multidisciplinary practice, with general dental care, orthodontics and oral surgery expertise within the team.

Get to know the practice ↗
Before your first visit

Start with
your questions.

You do not need to know the name of a treatment before contacting us. Tell the team what you would like help with.

Start an enquiry ↗

  1. Share what matters to you.

    Let the team know your concerns, whether you are arranging routine care, looking into a treatment or feeling nervous about a visit.

  2. Make room for understanding.

    Discuss your dental needs with the dentist. Ask about the assessment, suitable options and any investigations that may be needed.

  3. Ask about the next step.

    Before deciding on treatment, ask about alternatives, costs, appointment stages and what to expect afterwards.

come in.

Useful information to help you plan your visit.

How do I request an appointment?

Call +65 6222 2641 or WhatsApp +65 8132 2784. You can also use the official contact page . The clinic will confirm the appointment with you.

Do you provide dental care for children?

Newlife offers children's dentistry and early orthodontic services. Explore children's dental care , or contact the team to ask about arranging a visit.

Can I ask about replacing missing teeth?

Yes. Newlife provides information about dental implants and other restorative services. Your dentist will assess suitability and discuss the options relevant to you.
